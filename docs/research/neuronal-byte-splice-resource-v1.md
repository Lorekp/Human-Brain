# Typed byte-splice resource review v1

The selected topology is feasible to implement with modest retained weights and bounded activity banks. Its main new cost is scoring every raw workspace boundary, not learned parameter storage. These are exact shape/count calculations and source-derived lifetime recommendations, not measured speed or an RSS guarantee. No model, forward call, checkpoint, action, test, or timing probe was executed.

The reconciled bounds are four files, at most 128 raw bytes per file, request/path at most 31 raw bytes plus EOS, and payload at most 16 raw bytes plus EOS. There are at most 516 start candidates and 129 end candidates in the selected file. Widths are start 6177, end 2049, operation 4244 including the actual payload EOS flag. Start/end each share one weight vector across candidate applications; operation has two output vectors and two biases. Fresh action weights therefore total 16716 float32 values / 66864 bytes. The existing reader's 567568-byte tensor payload becomes 634432 bytes if otherwise unchanged. Four such models plus the previous 48 tapes would total 34069120 bytes, conditional on retaining that same future harness layout; this is not yet a selected new protocol payload.

Its two-output update need not create a candidate-axis reduction bank for one input.

Keep selector banks sequential. The free proposal discards its banks before external execution. Corrective SPLICE teaching forms fresh start/end/operation banks from the immutable pre-action workspace, with disclosed corrected prefix inputs; teacher STOP uses the actual proposal's operation features without fictitious span/payload labels. Stage the small proposed parameter updates until all required finite checks pass, then publish with the declared atomicity. Do not retain all free and teacher banks or multiply the peak by all four model arms unless the implementation actually owns them simultaneously.

Efficient exact forward construction is available without changing the neuronal graph. Compute raw QP-transpose and QF-transpose once per file, then QC-transpose per boundary; concatenate these raw components and offset, and normalize each whole 6177-vector to squared energy 32. Do not normalize blocks separately. At the maximum workspace this uses 17567744 fixed feature multiplications and 17018752 channel additions for start, versus 101978112 multiplications if invariant file blocks are needlessly recomputed for every boundary. Batched matmul/broadcast plus concatenation still materializes every presynaptic feature before learned scoring. It never substitutes reverse key-error transport. Floating reduction order must be fixed and checked against an independent scalar/vector oracle; algebraic equality alone is not promised bitwise parity.

End construction adds at most 8454144 feature multiplications / 8189952 channel additions; operation adds 135712 / 131471. Thus one maximum free action uses 26157600 fixed feature multiplications before learned scoring, excluding encoding, normalization and the existing payload reader. The three heads add 3460141 score multiplications and 3459494 score additions, plus operation biases, candidate softmaxes and decisions. A corrective SPLICE pass repeats its forward work and adds 3460141 local preactivity/error products, with 3443427 start/end candidate-reduction additions. These count actual operations; STOP teaching, fewer candidates or early payload EOS may do less. Report free and teacher work separately.

Physical ownership is three shared score circuits with one start, one end and two operation outputs, plus their explicitly represented candidate competition populations. Up to 516+129+2 candidate potentials are evaluated. The 3187332 start feature activities are stored/evaluated neuronal inputs, not 3187332 independently learned synapses. A fully materialized boundary-context bank alone adds 528384 float32 activities / 2113536 bytes. Query, four paths, four whole-file populations, selected span and payload add bounded smaller code banks; their 32-channel source codes are shared with the reader, not new learned embeddings.

Whole-feature energy 32 bounds the simple conditional start/end CE curvature by 32. Operation's bias augments its presynaptic vector to energy 33 (conservative bound); the unchanged eta .01 remains well inside the one-output-layer conditional descent allowance. This does not establish sequential action correctness, representation adequacy or generalization, and no downstream error may be propagated through a chosen index or into the reader.

The existing held codec still evaluates each occupied tape slot only once per call. Increasing the payload cap to 16 permits 17 actual mixture decisions and 16 gathers: at 128 slots this moves 14737408 response bytes per maximum call, while the three-bank overlap remains 2763264 bytes. Raw saved tensor steps at that cap total 87176 bytes for mixtures, priors, posteriors and cursors, before Python/JSON expansion. Free plus teacher full-vector receipts can exceed the old 262144-byte row-tree guard. Price the new receipt schema explicitly; do not silently inherit the old line/tree capacity or increase it after cost. Avoid journaling the multi-megabyte action feature banks: scalar/score/candidate/selection receipts plus independent ordinary arithmetic QA and snapshot custody can define an honest narrower audit scope.

Proceed with one implementation and authored correctness fixtures, not profiling or dimension/rate searches. Check candidate coverage and overflow rejection, scalar versus batched feature construction, whole-vector normalization, direct local updates/frozen controls/finite atomicity, and actual versus corrected-prefix receipts. Fresh complete cost must include all workspace scans, feature rebuilding, at-cap payload and receipt work, execution categories, and streamed audit parsing under the unchanged ceilings. V6 timing and memory headroom do not admit this new workload.
