# Repeated teaching of two integrated skills

**Root-reviewed and released for one bounded execution, 2026-09-10.**
The user's current instruction keeps architecture and task difficulty fixed,
allows bounded repeated teaching, and asks whether sufficient effective teaching
can establish complete acquisition before expanding retention or changed-context
evaluation. This supersedes the previous comparison's two-correction budget for
this proposed successor only. The completed comparison remains immutable.

The [roadmap](roadmap-reading-to-coding.md) and
[active plan](persistent-language-action-development-v1.md) own project direction.
The [previous learning review](stable-credit-v1-learning-review.md) motivates this
measurement: only seven of 35 answer-symbol offers selected the literal branch,
neither taught skill became correct, and a single late historical reading hit
had no later retention measurement. These facts do not prove that repetition
will solve the problem.

Complete every admitted block and scheduled panel even if an early response is

## Three separate measurements

**Effective coverage.** For every skill, repetition and target position, save the
actual target byte/EOS, prefix, context/gate/mode choice, literal inclusion,
pretarget distribution identity, target probability and stable literal CE when
evaluated, actual match, proposed update and publication gate. Aggregate external
offers, literal proposals and literal publications separately. Report per-position
coverage 0..32, unvisited positions, and minimum/median/maximum coverage; also
group repeated byte values separately without merging their positions. Keep raw
bytes and EOS separate. Three published positions on one occasion do not mean
the entire answer received direct literal teaching. Upstream credit applies to
all offered symbols under the existing rule and needs its own denominator.
Track motor label offers, selected/correct labels and actual published changes
separately from payload teaching. Nonzero proposed norms do not prove publication
or complete task learning.

**Intended-output probability.** Record before-publication teacher-prefix values
from actual training, and a separately declared read-only target-prefix diagnostic
at each boundary. They are different measurements: reader.teach holds weights
through the complete correction and publishes only afterward. Its own receipt
cannot measure that correction's post-update effect. A diagnostic must name the
checkpoint, raw observation, target prefix, actual state/RNG and conditioning.
No target may enter the forward activity before that position is evaluated.

The existing sampled-mode diagnostic is q=p(target) when LITERAL is selected and
q=actual deterministic match otherwise. Report literal target probability/CE,
q and sampled match rates with their complete denominators; do not call q a
full answer probability. The inherited prior_nll is another separate quantity.
The proposed pure helper captures held literal weights and evaluates the missing
literal distribution from saved activity, without a new live draw, prior call,
observe or teacher action. It combines all four mode branches, conditional on
the actual sampled context/gate and correct target prefix; it must validate the
held snapshot and activity identity. Any branches it cannot verify remain
unavailable or explicitly bounded. Do not multiply
sampled-mode q values or per-position averages into an alleged complete-answer
probability. A product/log score of the helper's full mode-mixture values must
be labeled conditional on that sampled target-prefix context/gate trajectory,
not marginalized complete free-output probability. Report EOS probability
separately from ordinary byte probability.
Diagnostic target-prefix calls and symbols count as evaluation exposure even
when no slow state is published; distinguish them from the 2,240 live teacher
symbols. If existing disabled teach is used, disclose its real teacher offers,
RNG advancement and required edit execution rather than labeling it exposure-free.

**Complete free outcomes.** Preserve every generated byte, prefix and stop reason,
including empty answers, premature EOS, non-EOS length limits and incorrect
complete responses. Reading success requires the exact accepted bytes and EOS.
Editing reports payload equality/EOS, operation, path/span, canonical-proposal
equality, accepted execution, raw stdout/stderr, actual after-files, driver
integrity and final task grade separately. Only an actual accepted valid edit
meeting the original task requirement demonstrates the integrated edit skill.
Teacher-forced correctness, payload alone, public exit zero or a successful
canonical teacher patch cannot substitute for the actor's free action.

## Acquisition, interference and stochastic uncertainty

Report a two-skill acquisition vector at every boundary, not a pooled byte score.
The acquisition criterion requires both taught tasks to be correct as complete
free responses at **both index 32 and final index 64**: exact accepted reading
bytes with EOS and a valid actual edit meeting its task grade. This is two
scheduled observations per skill, not a reliability estimate. All earlier,
control and historical outcomes remain reported. No expansion occurs within
this run even if the criterion passes. Acquisition on both tasks is necessary
before proposing expanded retention/changed-context work; it does not
automatically authorize that later execution.

R8 measures reading after eight reading corrections. E8 measures whether that
reading survives eight edit corrections while editing improves. Alternating
blocks measure coexistence under interleaved teaching. Compare both skills at
each boundary, including probability changes before either becomes correct.
A drop in a previously successful skill is a candidate interference observation,
not proof from a single stochastic failure. If there was no measured prior
success, report failure to acquire rather than forgetting. R02-R's previous
one-off late hit and R12-R's already absent behavior are not guaranteed positive
baselines for this continuation.

Keep live RNG uninterrupted. At each checkpoint, reset only the disposable
neural copies to the parent's original starting RNG before the four-case panel.
This is the declared common-initial-RNG panel, not a seed search. CP0 starts both
neural copies identically; later learned choices can diverge and consume
different numbers of draws, so it is not a claim of full-path matching.
Record actual incoming RNG, observation and internal trajectory hashes and
disclose later branch/draw divergence. Compare against the learning-disabled
control and report complete sample counts, gains and losses. With one free sample per
case/boundary, report descriptive variation; it cannot separate interference
from sampling variability or prove a durable retained skill.
