## Comparison and next use

[V2](persistent-language-action-v2-cost-review.md) took117.971022 seconds and
peaked at406.921875 MiB. V3's complete elapsed ratio is **0.5136619**, while its
serial maximum peak is512.5234375 MiB. The manifested requests/targets, prior,
learning rule, teacher exposure and retained receipts differ. V3 has344 teacher
offers versus196 in v2,2,249 free output events versus2,216, and no new ingests.
Neither this elapsed ratio nor the changed peak isolates the cache's effect.

There is substantial complete-budget headroom; no further speed experiment is
required to justify broader learning work. If sessions grow longer, a useful
host-memory improvement remains storing only metric fields in the runner's
in-memory history while preserving full append-only journal receipts. That
would address a source-visible retention mechanism without removing neuronal
work or evidence, but its benefit has not been measured here. It does not
resolve the capability failures.
