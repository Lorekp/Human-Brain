# Paired dendritic learning: ordinary integration QA

Both selected nodes pass on their first launch at
`cc4275969953e4f19da82d708bc3b926fb1da673`:12.73pytest seconds,
13.8375434outer seconds and763.66015625MiB observed owned-process peak under
the180-second/2048-MiB watchdog. These are local arithmetic and lifecycle checks,
not acquired language, fresh transfer, recovery or SWE results.

The [design](dendritic-request-binding-v1.md) uses paired basal/apical contacts
on the same byte/EOS cell branch. Basal activity represents ordered raw prefix;
apical activity represents the current raw request. Actual externally arriving
bytes teach their own sensory columns through bounded local permanence updates.
No reverse gradient or separately fitted executor is used. Old neuronal prior
tensors are retained and held while the new bank learns and acts.

## Core arithmetic

One isolated branch bank receives seven declared observed-symbol offers across
eight evaluations: two source offers, three enabled apical offers and two
disabled apical offers. Four valid standalone257-way tournaments compare every
actual level with an independent scalar lexicographic oracle; three invalid
inputs reject NaN, infinity and finite-value subtraction overflow. The cases
cover categorical priority, exact float64 near ties, odd carries and lower-byte
ties. Raw request and causal-prefix encodings are checked against scalar feature
identities, including empty input and the32-symbol boundary.

The local sequence is source `a`/EOS, apical attachment to the existing basal
branch, reinforcement and a conflicting observed byte. Positive contacts follow
their signed/clipped rule; false conditional predictions lose apical support
while basal contacts remain unchanged. Two explicitly authored permanence
fixtures exercise saturation and zero-underflow without extra learning rounds.
Disabled offers see an unchanged private bank and identical proposals. Malformed
strict state and damaged/replaced private stage storage reject without live
publication. There are five private stages and four commits, no actors, factual
tapes, children or checkpoint files.

## The same system learns and acts

Two fresh combined actors each receive `a` once through `ingest_with_receipt`:
four source-symbol offers total, with identical factual writes. Online learns
basal contacts; disabled computes and discards proposals without allocation
advancement. Neither source stream invokes the old response prior.

The disabled reader makes one response and receives a discarded `b`/EOS
correction. Online uses its learned source transitions to emit `a`/EOS during
its actual action proposal. Its selected STOP executes once over `print('a')`,
and the actual receipt returns to that same actor before a `b` SPLICE correction.
All inherited reader tensors remain unchanged; only new contact learning and
the action heads' own local correction may publish. This is a same-source
mechanics fixture with an explicitly authored EOS prior, not a transfer claim.

A deliberate late correction failure stages one EOS offer then rejects before
publication, preserving pending custody and live tensors. One accepted strict
in-memory load invalidates old private stages; one malformed load is rejected.
A NumPy mutation during a final free call is detected before pending state or
experience is published. The affected byte is restored and that fixture ends;
there is no successful retry or extra teacher pass.
