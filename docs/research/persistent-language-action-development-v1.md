## Purpose

Evaluate a persistent neuronal learner that reads ordinary language, retains
useful information across intervening work, acts on small real files, and uses
actual feedback to recover. Score supervised acquisition, genuinely fresh
transfer, delayed retention and teacher-free recovery separately. A successful
literal splice or local update is not sufficient evidence of language learning.

Use one small public development stream rather than another set of renamed
SET/KEEP pairs. Existing byte tasks are closed regression evidence, not a
perfection gate or a source of more endpoint-conditioned training. Do not wait
for every old toy to reach 100 percent before assessing broader usefulness.
This is not protected transfer, SWE-bench, a model-leaderboard comparison or a
claim of general software engineering.

## Teacher-free recovery is its own test

Each recovery episode begins with an ordinary issue and workspace. The learner
gets at most three action/verification attempts. A successful first attempt is
reported as such, not as recovery. After a genuine failure, only public tool
output, resulting files and declared non-oracular success/failure signals may
enter the next observation/state. Private grading and desired patches remain
unavailable. Do not replace a first action to manufacture an easy failure.

Public test feedback may be used, with its exact text exposed to every route;
hidden tests may score privately but never supply corrective hints. If no
first-attempt failures occur, the conditional recovery fraction is undefined,
not 100 percent. Report the raw denominator and the unconditional episode success.

The current finish_without_teaching method only resolves custody. With unchanged
request/tape/model, the reader deterministically repeats its payload even if the
workspace changed. Repeating that call is not a recovery mechanism. A recovery claim requires that the selected
implementation actually incorporate new public observation or perform a
declared local consequence update before another attempt. No reverse-mode
gradient, hidden correct span, teacher payload or separately trained repair
model may be concealed in that signal. If neither path exists,
keep slow weights fixed, expose the repeated failure and report zero recovery;
do not add a hidden feedback solver or defer the stream until recovery succeeds.
