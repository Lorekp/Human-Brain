# Public persistent language/action development

Status: root-selected broader development implementation, 2026-09-06.
The byte-coincidence comparison is completed. The proposed five-arm operation
successor is stopped; its unexecuted source is not the next experiment. This
document specifies the smallest broader development return using the current
core. It does not itself launch tasks, load models or authorize a separate fit. The roadmap owns
selection and implementation order.

## Purpose

Evaluate a persistent neuronal learner that reads ordinary language, retains
useful information across intervening work, acts on small real files, and uses
actual feedback to recover. Score supervised acquisition, genuinely fresh
transfer, delayed retention and teacher-free recovery separately. A successful
literal splice or local update is not sufficient evidence of language learning.

Use one small public development stream rather than another set of renamed
SET/KEEP pairs. Existing byte tasks are closed regression evidence, not a
perfection gate or a source of more endpoint-conditioned training. Do not wait
for every old toy to reach 100 percent before assessing broader usefulness.
This is not protected transfer, SWE-bench, a model-leaderboard comparison or a
claim of general software engineering.

## Smallest proposed stream

Freeze 56 episodes in this order, with no repeated rounds or selected checkpoints:

| Phase | Episodes | Teaching | Primary measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supervised acquisition | 24: 12 language, 12 executable actions | One declared correction after the first actual response/action | First-attempt accuracy and changes across three consecutive blocks of eight |
| Fresh transfer | 16: eight language, eight executable actions | None | First-attempt accuracy on withheld wording families and structural combinations |
| Teacher-free recovery | Eight executable issues, at most three actual attempts each | No desired answer/edit or hidden-test feedback | First-attempt success, recovery among first failures, and success within budget |
| Delayed retention | Eight: four language, four executable actions | None | Retrieval/use of earlier knowledge after all intervening episodes |

There is one persistent model per learned route, not a freshly fitted model per
task. Its learned parameters and eligible episodic memory survive all phases.
Task workspace resets or continued project state follow the frozen session
manifest, identically across routes. The manifest must identify which episodes
share a project and which only share earlier knowledge. No successful checkpoint
is selected retrospectively. A failed or invalid output consumes its attempt.

The supervised phase offers at most 24 corrections, one per episode. Language
corrections contain the declared answer/marking information; action corrections
contain the declared edit/response. Every offer, target byte, corrected action
field and actual synaptic publication is counted. There is no automatic
teacher-forcing replay until success, eight-round memorization or separate
executor training. A local learner may use a declared replay mechanism only in
a separately selected protocol with its extra exposure and cost.

## Public content and transfer criteria

Use a small collection of ordinary prose notes and a stdlib-only Python project
with real functions, configuration and tests. Tasks should require reading and
using requirements, not copying an isolated value from a formulaic record.
Include retrieving a stated fact, following a changed requirement, distinguishing
a condition or exception, relating two notes, and applying a requirement in code.
The executable subset should include at least a behavior change, a conditional
case, a configuration-to-behavior relation and a repair informed by a real error.
Do not supply an action template or canonical span as part of the observation.

Instruction-form holdout is by wording/construction family, not identifier:
reserve at least four complete forms for fresh transfer, including questions,
indirect requests and a condition/exception construction. Reserve combinations
of known requirements with new function/configuration placements or changed
ordering. Mere path renaming, a one-character key substitution or shifting all
whitespace is insufficient. Freeze these partitions before learner outputs;
report lexical and structural overlap rather than calling the public corpus
secret. No learner-specific task selection follows an observed error.

The first stream uses explicitly developer-authored public exercises, with
source revision and complete reproducible data. It makes no official-exam reading
claim. Authentic released reading questions and official marking guidance remain
separate subsequent evaluation pressure, not a prerequisite for exposing the
learner to this broader stream. When external material is selected, record and
verify its URL, revision and license; never invent provenance or crop a passage
around the target answer to make it fit.

Longer memory means a shared retained information stream with intervening,
potentially confusable material. The learner must determine relevance. Do not
hand it a task-selected tape containing only the requested record. Early facts
used in retention are removed from the current observation and must survive in
the model's declared memory mechanism. Their source bytes and every later
re-presentation remain in the exposure ledger. External generic storage is
permitted only if disclosed and equally available to matched controls; a host
lookup that already resolves the answer is an explicit retrieval baseline.

## Executable observation/action contract and visible capacity limits

Start with the banked current learner and its real bounds: requests at most 31
raw bytes, replacements/reader answers at most 16 bytes, at most four files of
128 bytes each, and 128 occupied memory slots. Natural short questions and new
instruction phrasings fit within these bounds; there is no requirement to use
SET/KEEP syntax. Short edits can change an assignment, return expression, list
entry or call argument, including a condition already present in the visible
program. No general program synthesis is claimed from these literal edits.

Root selects one generic growth of the same shared tape from 128 to 512 slots.
Review its exact all-address storage/work bounds and ordinary affected QA; keep
lag32, request31, payload16 and file128 limits. Do not make the broader stream wait for
a simultaneous 256-byte instruction, 2-KiB active memory, 256-byte output and
large-file rewrite. Every supported memory prefix must fit 512 occupied slots;
the full accumulated prefix, rather than a target-specific package, remains
available to the learner throughout the session.

Most episodes should fit the selected bounds. Reserve four fresh transfer items
(two language, two action) as clearly marked over-capacity challenges: longer
raw instructions, source context or required edits. The exact exceeded dimension
must be frozen in the roster. Feed the unchanged public input to the ordinary
interface and preserve its rejection or unsupported result as a visible failure;
never truncate, simplify to SET, select the answer-containing tape or omit these
items from overall totals. Also report the supported subset separately. These
challenges measure a limitation, not a reason to postpone all broader exposure.

Keep one persistent shared information stream, not a task-selected memory. The
public source bank must fit the selected occupied-slot capacity for supported
items; report every raw byte/EOS and all later observations that append or replace
memory. Earlier facts are tested after intervening episodes and source removal.
Capacity rejection is not forgetting, and these outcomes receive separate labels.

Use the raw request and workspace through the existing declared APIs, with actual
reader bytes explicitly recorded as the language response on language episodes.
For action episodes the same neuronal system chooses path, span, replacement and
operation; a host validates/applies that choice and captures genuine execution.
A host must not interpret a request, choose the correct fields, repair invalid
output or privately select a subtask. Exact same-instance action-before-teacher
custody, direct local rules, snapshots and qualified execution remain reusable.

Public feedback can be stored in the episode record now. Do not imply it enters
the neuronal reader unless the selected implementation actually consumes it.
Current payload generation sees request/tape, not arbitrary workspace or tool
text. The older persistent coding wrapper uses pointer inventories/materialization
and cannot silently replace this core. Its record conventions may be reused,
not its separately fitted solver. No new observation encoder or recovery rule
is a prerequisite for running the first broader stream: zero recovery is valid
negative evidence.

## Teacher-free recovery is its own test

Each recovery episode begins with an ordinary issue and workspace. The learner
gets at most three action/verification attempts. A successful first attempt is
reported as such, not as recovery. After a genuine failure, only public tool
output, resulting files and declared non-oracular success/failure signals may
enter the next observation/state. Private grading and desired patches remain
unavailable. Do not replace a first action to manufacture an easy failure.

Public test feedback may be used, with its exact text exposed to every route;
hidden tests may score privately but never supply corrective hints. If no
first-attempt failures occur, the conditional recovery fraction is undefined,
not 100 percent. Report the raw denominator and the unconditional episode success.

The current finish_without_teaching method only resolves custody. With unchanged
request/tape/model, the reader deterministically repeats its payload even if the
workspace changed. Repeating that call is not a recovery mechanism. A recovery claim requires that the selected
implementation actually incorporate new public observation or perform a
declared local consequence update before another attempt. No reverse-mode
gradient, hidden correct span, teacher payload or separately trained repair
model may be concealed in that signal. If neither path exists,
keep slow weights fixed, expose the repeated failure and report zero recovery;
do not add a hidden feedback solver or defer the stream until recovery succeeds.

## Matched references and interpretable outcomes

Use the online learner and one matched slow-learning-disabled copy with identical
initial state, observation/tool access, persistent episodic writes, budgets and
ordering. Freeze all slow publications in the disabled copy; do not also remove
its memory and then attribute the entire difference to learning. Declare every
initial parameter prior and its earlier exposure. Adding an LLM or external
knowledge system would require a separately labeled hybrid condition and an
external-component-only reference; none is silently selected here.

Use two transparent simple references on their corresponding subsets: a generic
lexical retrieval reference over the same visible/retained documents for language,
and nearest-acquisition-example literal edit reuse for code. Freeze their
similarity rule, tie handling and exact-application/rejection rule before running.
They may store the same supervised corrections. They receive no answer-based
document routing or handcrafted parser for withheld forms. Their outputs/actions
are scored exactly like the candidate; their host computation and storage are
disclosed. These references are conventional baselines, not neuronal evidence.

Publish raw counts and per-episode outcomes for all four phases. For language,
use the frozen factual rubric and official marking guidance where applicable,
with a deterministic scorer or blinded answer review rather than a model judging
its own answers. For actions, score actual final behavior and protected file
integrity rather than a canonical patch string. Report tool failures, invalid
outputs, no-ops, attempts and regressions. A small public development score is
descriptive evidence, not a statistical or protected capability claim.

The useful advancement question is whether the online learner improves fresh
language/actions beyond its disabled control and the applicable simple reference,
retains earlier useful knowledge, and recovers from some actual failures without
teachers. Require these dimensions to be shown separately; do not average reading
failure away with edits or label a successful supervised replay as transfer.
No all-toy perfection threshold follows. Freeze any numeric advancement threshold
only after the public source/roster and scorer are selected, before any learner
execution; otherwise report the result as descriptive development evidence.

## Complete budget and execution

The proposed schedule is 56 episode starts per learned route: 24 supervised,
16 fresh, eight recovery, eight retention. With at most three attempts only in
recovery, it permits at most 72 responses/actions per route. Of these, at most
48 are actual executable verifications (12 acquisition + eight fresh + 24
recovery + four retention); the other 24 are language responses. Count reference
calls, supervised targets, all retry observations, persistent storage, public
test execution, scoring and independent audit. No hidden warmed-up fit or
precomputed candidate answers enter the budget.

Use the existing monitored-process patterns and selected environment. Review a
complete prospective 900-second / 2048-MiB envelope, including model, capacity,
memory ingest, real tools, references and independent audit, before the first
workload run. Actual clock metadata, source/data/initial-state hashes, process
tree peaks, failures and all spent exposure must be preserved. Do not reuse toy
projection arithmetic whose populations and retries no longer describe this
stream. A bounded cost admission may price the newly declared complete workload;
its exact probe exposure must be frozen before execution, not tuned until it fits.

Next: implement the selected 512-slot capacity and 56-episode scope; the team records
authored data provenance and writes the smallest harness around the banked core,
with meaningful ordinary transport/data QA. Broader execution need not await a
new feature architecture or successful recovery mechanism. No further operation
microbenchmark, toy cohort or standalone fit is a prerequisite. Unsupported challenge outcomes and failed recovery are visible results. Preserve
them, complete the bounded supported stream, and use its failures to choose any
later generic change; do not return to another byte-only perfection queue.
