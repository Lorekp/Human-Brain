# Persistent language/action V5: saved learning review

Online correction produces one delayed exact reading success, no fresh success
and no successful action or recovery. It also collapses much of the output onto
the newly taught reading answer `idle`: 61 of 80 responses, including 45 of 52 action
payloads and all 24 recovery attempts. Those responses already have coherent
sequence continuation and termination. Choosing a relevant sequence, and avoiding
broad interference from local routing updates, is the more immediate problem.

The complete run and independent saved audit pass on their first launches at
released source `c2b3f92`: 558.5371355 seconds / 723.078125 MiB for execution and
8.3754013 seconds / 102.6640625 MiB for the separate audit. The
[frozen protocol](persistent-language-action-v5-protocol.md) owns this continuation.
All 64 episodes are now exposed development material.

Both neuronal routes continue independent copies of the actual V4 online model,

Eight correction occasions offer 155 raw bytes plus 8 EOS = 163 symbols per neuronal

current-correction comparison, not four instances of historical forgetting.
Simple retains eight earlier successes and improves two. Prior regressions
compare saved actual last attempts with the current authored reset; their
observation-equality and age fields remain explicit in the independent report.

## What the local correction actually does

All 163 online teacher events recruit a new assembly, giving 398 occupied owners.
There are no exact teacher reuses or exhausted offers. The inherited 235 outgoing
vectors are therefore not overwritten by this stream. At every offer the routed
pre-clamp owner differs from the separately prepared new owner whose output is
taught. The output clamp stores the offered byte/EOS in that new event; it does
not train the already routed event to become the correct sequence.

Online receives 18 positive and 145 negative routing outcomes; disabled computes
13 positive and 150 negative outcomes. Mean pre-clamp teacher NLL is 5.62597 versus
5.79607. All 163 online routing proposals stage and none skips for zero activity;
disabled stages none. The selected-row local arithmetic processes 89,269 values
online and 101,927 disabled. The independent audit checks the actual own-emission
comparison, scalar current clamp, outgoing 257-current update and publication
accounting. It does not reconstruct the omitted million-wide theta updates.

Only three online positive outcomes are whitespace/punctuation/digit bytes under
the extractor's disclosed byte set; all three are spaces. One further positive
outcome is EOS. Several remaining positives are shared letters of `return` from
a different code offer. Reusing those bytes can be legitimate. The data therefore
do not establish that rewards for spaces or EOS alone caused the collapse.
The positive EOS at `A03-E` position 34 comes from `A03-R`'s EOS event at position 4:
the correct current symbol does not identify an appropriate complete sequence.

Online free and teacher trajectories share ten actual prefix states; disabled
shares eight. Memory winners agree at every shared state. Online's owner agrees
on eight of the ten, with the other two occurring after earlier private teacher
updates. These are changed staged parameters under a supplied causal prefix,
not evidence that future teacher bytes entered the prior actual response.

## Observed reweighting and its causal limits

There are 62 online/control pairs with an identical first-step raw routing-context
hash: all 28 readings and 34 edits. Their first selected owner differs on 61 pairs.
For those identical inputs, the same 235 inherited prototype views have the same
fixed baseline and feature construction. Comparing their actually recorded
currents thus exposes the effect of the changed shared routing weights without
reconstructing a feature vector or generating a counterfactual response.

The four readings that disabled answers correctly provide a concrete example:

| Prior case | Disabled correct entry | Its disabled current | Its online current | Actual online rank |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
|R08-R|0|0.24444|-0.79433|15|
|R10-R|49|0.24444|-0.74234|15|
|R12-R|155|0.24444|-0.74469|15|
|R14-R|177|0.24444|-0.79310|15|

Every one of the 235 inherited owners has a lower online current in all four
contexts; the median shift is approximately −2.51 to−2.53. The new `idle` entry 358
wins with a current between −0.159 and−0.131. No winner ties occur in either
neuronal route's saved decisions. Tie orientation is not an observed cause here.

This establishes broad shared-current interference and changed selection, not
destruction of old output storage. It does not isolate interference from the
additional 163 competitors: routing updates and recruitment occur together.
Nor do scalar scores identify which lexical or metadata coordinates caused the
change. The source has a concrete risk worth examining: signed flag differences
reverse sign when current/candidate roles reverse, so a negative local update
can contribute a positive metadata current to a reversed mismatch. An exact
complete-context swap also has identical commutative lexical products: with
active lexical blocks these cancel that boost at equal positions, and the total
effect is nonpositive at unequal positions. A total boost therefore needs a
different lexical context or another explicitly derived condition. Source algebra alone cannot identify its
dominance in this run, and no missing feature vectors were reconstructed.

## Next shared priority

Defer a continuity latch or transition-bank implementation. The dominant observed
failure is broad, inappropriate sequence relevance despite coherent storage and
stopping. The single next priority is to make local routing credit more specific
to the public instruction/context relation, so penalizing one evaluation does
not broadly favor an opposing or unrelated relation. A bounded source/math
review of the signed metadata and shared-credit geometry is the appropriate
decision input; a representation change is not yet selected by this review.
It must preserve direct local learning, target-free ownership and the same actor,
without teacher episode labels, reverse error transport or a separate executor.

Broader language/source learning remains important but is a different exposure
claim. Current document writes do not train output syntax. Future once-only
prediction of newly arriving source symbols could supply more natural experience
while reducing answer corrections, with all targets and capacity pressure counted.
That is not authorization to replay these documents, add a hidden corpus, repeat
the 163 answers or insert a toy-perfection gate. This completed stream supports
neither useful fresh composition nor teacher-free recovery.
