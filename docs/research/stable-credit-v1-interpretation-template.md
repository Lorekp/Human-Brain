# Stable credit comparison: interpretation template

2026-09-09. Written while the frozen comparison at
`5622f600921e6811d66254e25c8e956a31cd19ab` ran. This is an interpretation aid,
not a new prelaunch registration or a
change to the [fixed protocol](stable-language-action-credit-comparison-v1.md).
The protocol owns cases, teaching, checkpoints, custody and resource limits.
It makes no claim of preregistration independent of the ongoing development conversation.

## Evidence gate

Record completion, audit status, source/manifest identities and actual resource
use first. If a worker, scheduled battery, preservation check or audit is
incomplete, report its observed prefix and missing denominators. Do not infer a
complete old/new learning comparison from it. A paired CP0 behavior mismatch is
a migration failure. Equal failures establish preserved behavior, not retained
skill; an anchor already absent in old CP0 was not lost during this migration.

## Fill every dimension independently

For each row below, show old-enabled, causal-enabled, their respective disabled
routes and both actual reference copies at CP0, CP16, CP32 and CP48. Include
individual cases, complete denominators and enabled-minus-disabled and
causal-enabled-minus-old-enabled differences. Preserve reading/action splits.

| Dimension | Required interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition | Show both taught skills separately, including their actual responses before correction. Later correct exact-task output is an observed acquisition outcome; it does not establish changed-context transfer. |
| Retention of taught skills | Show earlier-correct to later-correct/lost transitions, persistent failures and gains, plus delayed main encounters. Give actual attempts, source symbols and public scan symbols since correction. A skill never correct has no demonstrated retention to lose. |
| Historical retention | Show every anchor with its historical source route, old CP0 outcome and later outcomes. Separate migration loss, already absent behavior and loss during the new stream. Do not count restoration of one anchor as preservation of all fourteen. |
| Changed context | Keep wording, values/context and structural combinations separate; include every declared main-stream stratum and actual project descendant. Values/context cases also change wording. These are repeatedly exposed developmental outcomes, without fresh-test or isolated-factor claims. |
| Recovery | Report first failures, later and final correctness, actual accepted edits, protected-file integrity and changed/repeated proposals. Distinguish successful later repair from a changed proposal, exit zero or generic later task success. Retain real diagnostic/context divergence; repair alone does not isolate the causal contribution of reading feedback. |

Keep diagnostic measures—symbol matches, conditional rewards, literal CE,
inclusion counts, response length/EOS and update work—separate from task success.
Improved local objectives with unchanged behavior do not establish useful task
learning. Report complete costs alongside outcomes; valid custody or faster
journaling is not a neuronal capability gain.

## Decision language after the evidence is filled

| Observed pattern | Permitted conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| No task advantage over old-enabled and the corresponding disabled route | No useful advantage of the selected credit change demonstrated on this fixed workload, even if diagnostic objectives improve. |
| Gains on some skills/checkpoints with losses elsewhere | Mixed result: name the exact gains, losses and their timing. Do not collapse the tradeoff into a single improvement claim or select a best checkpoint. |
| Better acquisition retained later, with advantages over old and disabled controls | Bounded evidence of useful learning on those named skills in this run. State changed-context and recovery outcomes independently; neither follows from acquisition. |
| Reference succeeds where neural routes fail | The task is achievable by the disclosed retrieval/editing reference; this does not show neuronal learning. Its retained text/demonstration history remains a material comparison difference. |

At later checkpoints, separate descriptive same-roster score differences from
comparisons with matching actual observations and pre-attempt RNG. List unpaired
cases rather than replace diagnostics or reseed. Reference copies establish
complete parity only if every required case pairs and agrees; agreement on the
paired subset is narrower and is not independent replication.

Close with the supported per-dimension findings and unresolved limits of this
single fixed, exposed run. Do not invent statistical significance, broader skill
coverage or generalization from two taught skills. Keep task difficulty and the
teaching budget fixed. No outcome or QA pass automatically selects another
mechanism, replay, resource increase or curriculum change.
