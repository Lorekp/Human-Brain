retained in the artifact. Raw-score and affine choices/outcomes agree on all
52 updating-arm probes. Materialized edits match selected actions, decoys remain
unchanged, and evaluation supplies no live documents or learning feedback.

Each same-seed frozen mate receives its arm's 300 feedback events and has zero
native or learned-state changes. Frozen affine solves no supported fresh or old
task and correctly STOPs on both unsupported requests. The external cosine
reference solves no supported fresh or old task and acts wrongly on unsupported
requests. Both controls share the previously learned neuronal literal-edit motor;
they isolate evidence learning rather than the contribution of the whole system.

The frozen MiniLM encoder, retained raw documents, candidate vectors and external
correction ledger remain disclosed components. Source absence from live input is
not source erasure or semantic compression. The conventional global AROW update
changes direction and magnitude together; native placement is not biological
discovery, and this comparison does not isolate covariance from step size.

and two items pending official-rubric human scoring, not a finalized 0/2 score.
These repeated items are regression observations; fresh official reading material
