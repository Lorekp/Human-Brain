## Interpretation and closure

Qualification, tag-specific advantage and current-rule advantage are all false.
Tagged neither beats the immediate predecessor nor the writer-frozen control on
any endpoint stratum. Aggregate loses one fit success relative to its prior
query-credit result. The reviewed conditional value derivative and passing
numerical QA therefore do not establish useful learned writing, fresh binding,
convergence or a reason to extend this fixed fit. Preserve this negative result;
no additional fit, epoch sweep or automatic feedback change follows.

Qualification required tagged 8/8 fit and 4/4 new-value, delayed and interference,
with strict new-value advantage over writer-frozen, frozen, writes-disabled and
immediate-predecessor tagged. These are already exposed synthetic development
combinations. The source-free route excludes document/target input to inference;
it is not a filesystem sandbox. Read-only distractors are not neuronal decay or
retention through later teaching. The host-authored occurrence motor supplies the
narrow action schema. No general reading, neuronal action-policy, protected coding
or benchmark-comparison claim follows. The next bounded binding review is a
separate decision; this record authorizes no new model execution.
