# Compact versus varied teaching result

Completed public TRAIN development, 2026-09-05. **Keep compact teaching with
RESERVED allocation; do not adopt VARIED.** The [frozen protocol](contextual_curriculum_protocol.md)
requires greater fresh execution without regression in either language stratum,
common retention or unsupported STOPs. Fresh totals tie, and familiar-form
performance regresses. This comparison is closed; it does not justify another
wording, rate or epoch sweep.

All primary outcomes use the fixed affine neuronal action path.

| Familiar-form fresh edits | 1/4 | 0/4 |

The fresh difference is one gain and one loss: VARIED gains hupi audit
(`hupi` → `calendula`) and loses gaxo transport (`larkspur` → `audit`). Both
solve ivra transport. Retention gains falu transport and horo audit but loses
abri and itva transport. These paired changes matter despite tied totals.
Neither policy reaches 6/8 fresh, 12/16 retained and three complete fresh entities.
All 48 teacher repairs per arm pass; they are scaffolding, not autonomous recovery.

The added read-only diagnostics separate acquisition from query-form transfer:

| Raw-selection probe | COMPACT | VARIED |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual taught questions, immediately after teaching | 5/16 | 8/16 |
| Common original questions, same checkpoint | 5/16 | 5/16 |

These are diagnostic selections, not additional executed successes. Switching
VARIED to the common questions loses five answers and gains two at the same
checkpoint. Source knowledge and weights have not changed, demonstrating
query-form sensitivity. Fresh ingestion then displaces cevu audit (`flint` →
`jomu`) under COMPACT and gemi audit (`ruby` → `jomu`) under VARIED. Source
competition can therefore cause a further failure without synaptic forgetting.

Every final negative pair in each arm attained unit margin at some earlier offer.
The largest last-offer-to-final margin drop is 33.89 under COMPACT and 61.16
under VARIED. Final minimum margins are −14.42 and −25.60. This is measured
interference in actual learned contrasts; it does not prove a representation
capacity limit. The earlier [TRAIN-only feasibility witness](contextual_representation_feasibility_result.md)
remains excluded and says nothing about the new fresh queries.

Of eleven wrong COMPACT actual-taught-query leaders, five were admitted negatives
and six were never executed for that exact query/occurrence. VARIED's eight
wrong taught-query leaders split four and four. VARIED abri audit fails despite
all of its recorded pairs fitting; dofi audit has no admitted pair but is correct.
All VARIED common-probe exact-query pair counts are zero because its teaching
questions differ. This does not mean the facts were never taught; common probes
must not inherit certified contrasts merely through matching case names.

Both policies fail the two-word fresh targets. COMPACT selects `holly` and `flax`
instead of `sea holly` and `blue flax`, and another answer includes `bear speedwell`.
VARIED selects incidental words or an old value on these cases. Selection and
span boundaries remain weak; prior successful two-word execution established
that the existing motor can carry such values. All 52 updating raw-score/affine
choices agree, with no primary STOP or motor refusal. Frozen affine and external
references solve no supported fresh or retained task; frozen controls correctly
STOP on the unsupported requests.

## Evidence integrity and limits

Reviewed source and all fixtures were frozen at
`681928bce9d8ea7a48f4c616ccb5f22b6edc7002`. One sequential COMPACT/VARIED run
completed with exit zero and no retries. The only shared harness changes route
per-arm teaching fixtures, record actual-query probes and require exact query
identity when attributing executed negatives. Core/native code, learning rule,
features, motor and original teaching/evaluation loops remain unchanged.

Inert actual-source QA verifies routing, once-only ingestion, common probes,
default behavior, query-specific attribution, strict result serialization,
output guards and failure preservation. Fixture review verifies the same sixteen
facts and encounter order, unique reachable teacher values and shared fresh
strata. Ruff, compilation and diff checks pass; unaffected native suites were
not repeated.

All 36 declared source hashes, native/encoder identities and saved arrays verify.
Independent scalar reconstruction reproduces both arms' 150 updater and 150
frozen offers, complete receipts, checkpoint lineage and final weights exactly.
Gap reconstruction error is zero; maximum roundoff in the algebraic gap formula
is 1.07e-14.
COMPACT's four saved arrays are bit-exact to the prior RESERVED checkpoint.
Frozen learned state stays zero. All 572 transfer/retention executions preserve
decoys and materialize the selected action with no live source documents.
Actual TRAIN and common probes use their declared queries without ingesting
COMPACT source bytes into VARIED; evaluation is read-only.

Raw documents, contextual vectors and the frozen MiniLM encoder are external
components retained internally. Live source absence is not raw-source erasure
or semantic compression. The controls share the learned literal-edit motor and
isolate evidence selection, not the whole neuronal system. The intervention
changes source wording, queries, candidates and on-policy negatives together;
it neither isolates one linguistic feature nor establishes a general claim that
diverse teaching cannot help.

Original official KS2 Q6a/b runs once per arm with no feedback. COMPACT emits
`vibrations` twice; VARIED emits `increase` twice. Each has zero confirmed marks
and two items pending official-rubric human scoring, not a finalized 0/2.
These repeated questions are regression observations. Authored transfer cases
provide no independent reading, protected evaluation or roadmap-gate pass.
