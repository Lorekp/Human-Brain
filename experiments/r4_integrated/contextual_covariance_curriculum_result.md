both strict fresh improvement and common-retention nonregression. The separate
fresh, retention, complete-entity and integrated targets are not reached here.
The prior fixed-rule curriculum rejection and AROW adoption remain unchanged.

## Failure localization

At an unchanged trained checkpoint, switching VARIED from actual teaching
questions to common questions loses nine correct selections and gains one:
13/16 becomes 5/16. COMPACT uses identical actual/common questions and remains
14/16 at that point. Read-only selection probes are not extra executed successes.

Fresh ingestion then reduces COMPACT's common selections from 14/16 to 11/16:
cevu audit (`flint`) and gemi audit (`ruby`) select `vyni`, while horo transport
(`talc`) selects `timetable`. VARIED remains at five correct with no displacement
of a correct answer, although some wrong selections change. Learned weights and
covariance stay byte-identical during ingestion and evaluation. This is new
candidate competition, not a weight-forgetting event. COMPACT's repeated trained
checkpoint exactly matches the prior adopted AROW checkpoint; its lower result
on this new cohort does not invalidate that reproduction.

not another wording, value-length, teaching-dose or stability sweep. They do not
prove that the current representation is mathematically incapable of transfer.
