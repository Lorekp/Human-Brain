# Unchanged-policy robustness result

Completed public TRAIN development, 2026-09-05. **Keep RESERVED; do not adopt
PROTECTED.** The [frozen protocol](contextual_robustness_protocol.md) requires a
retention gain without fresh or unsupported regression in every stream.
PROTECTED fails that rule in wording and questions. Its retention benefit is
consistent across these contexts, but neither policy meets the integrated targets.
The earlier [failed adoption verdict](contextual_protected_contrast_result.md)
also remains unchanged. This three-stream comparison is closed.

correct `kestrel` becomes the cross-sentence span `kestrel. The audit`; other
losses select an old value, an incidental verb or another fresh entity. Five
of seven wrong PROTECTED answers have the gold span above STOP but below the
selected wrong span. Threshold adjustment alone would not repair these rankings.

Questions loses pire audit (`cypress` becomes the old value `ruby`). Both
policies succeed on obnu and roki transport; PROTECTED solves no audit request.
Other errors select the wrong property or incidental words from the right source.
The results expose relation and span ranking failures without proving that the
paraphrase alone causes them.

genuinely repeats uvra's `reed bunting` fact; this is an incomplete span from

## Next capability decision

Keep RESERVED, fixed updates and affine actions. Close unchanged-policy testing;
do not tune protection or repeat recertification on these cohorts. The fixed
contextual encoder already supplies contextual token vectors and the selector
uses query/span interactions, so these failures do not establish that contextual
features are absent. Teaching repeatedly presents compact facts and a narrow
question set; downstream sources add clauses, distractions and varied questions.
Teaching diversity is a plausible bottleneck, not a demonstrated cause.

The [active plan](../../docs/research/balanced-evidence-to-action-session-v1.md)
therefore selects one bounded comparison of compact versus varied teaching with
the existing RESERVED learner and matched fact/feedback budgets. Freeze a new
transfer roster and acceptance rule before outputs. Test acquisition, transfer,
retention and unsupported behavior together; do not fit spent fresh labels or
change features, learning rate and teaching distribution at once. Authentic
official-exam expansion needs its own manifest under the reading plan. No roadmap
or protected gate is passed by this result.
