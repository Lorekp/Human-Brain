## What the failure localizes

All whole teacher answers were available in the fixed candidate inventory.
Every scheduled offer scores every candidate plus STOP and recomputes the current
gradient. All 511 updates reduce their immediately taught lesson's recorded loss;
that local improvement does not establish aggregate acquisition or retention.
Every update is clipped. Across passes, mean pre-offer loss for new supported
lessons improves from 7.08984 to 6.09920, while new STOP lessons worsen from
7.54986 to 8.95487. The learner does not simply need previously omitted competitors
to be admitted into the objective.

Before growth, supported actual queries have median authorized-target probability
0.002514 and rank 25. Unsupported STOP has median probability 0.0001143 and ranks
1457–1459 among 1459 outputs. The final learned STOP scalar is 0.0409081095.
These observations motivate checking objective scale and interference; they do
not establish a representational impossibility or prove which replacement works.

A bounded forensic analysis attributes exactly the sixteen observed unsupported
wrong-leader gaps through the saved 511 increments, without searching for new
candidates or executing another model. There are 127 STOP offers (64 new,
63 replayed) and 384 supported offers. STOP lessons add 0.04100744 to the STOP
scalar; supported lessons subtract only 0.00009933. Scalar subtraction alone
therefore does not explain its poor relative ranking.

The mean final candidate-minus-STOP gap is **3.72721**: supported lessons contribute
+10.89094 and STOP lessons -7.16373. The direct and joint relative-token blocks
(H and F) dominate, with net contributions +2.36662 and +1.41238; all other blocks

Of 46 wrong supported actual choices, nine are partial answers and 37 other spans;

Alternate wording has 13 partial-answer errors and 30 other-span errors.

All 1,024 recorded raw/affine comparisons agree. Supported selection correctness
and execution outcomes agree. The immediate blocker is acquisition, including

## Return to acquisition diagnosis

The next bounded question is whether the existing fixed features can jointly
satisfy the 64 actual TRAIN requests, including whole-span answers and STOP.
Use the saved basis and declared target sets for a host-only feasibility and
conditioning diagnostic, with a declared solver/tolerance, ranking-margin
contract and at most 30 minutes. Margin infeasibility concerns that contract;
it does not exclude every action policy that could abstain through ties.
Do not deploy its weights or count its fits as neuronal acquisition. A feasible
fit would focus the next design on acquisition/credit and interference; a valid
infeasibility certificate would justify revisiting representation. A timeout or
failed optimization proves neither. Compare one next design only after that
distinction, retaining explicit exact-output supervision and matched controls.

No extra epochs, rate/label sweeps, projection or trainable producer follows
automatically. The current record remains immutable; a new design needs a
representative preflight and its own declared contract. Fresh integration,
official reading and protected gates retain their separate rules.
