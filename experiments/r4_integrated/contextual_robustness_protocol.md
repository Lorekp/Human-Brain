# Unchanged-policy robustness across three fresh source contexts

Public TRAIN development contract, 2026-09-05. Freeze all three fixtures,
runner and this protocol before any model output. This is a bounded comparison
of existing policies, not a new learning mechanism or protected evaluation.

## Observed failure, question and decision

The [protected-contrast result](contextual_protected_contrast_result.md) improves
retained execution from 5/16 to 9/16 but reduces fresh execution from 6/8 to 5/8.
It fails its original adoption rule. All nineteen protected contrasts survive;
remaining failures involve uncertified distinctions and unencountered competitors,
including one correct old answer displaced by fresh ingestion at unchanged weights.

Test whether the retention advantage and fresh-performance tradeoff persist when
source wording, question form and competition broaden beyond renaming. This
comparison evaluates the robustness of unchanged learned policies. It does not
identify the causal effect of any one language feature: the streams contain
different entities, values and documents as well as different wording.

RESERVED remains the baseline unless PROTECTED has **strictly more retained
successes and no fewer supported fresh successes in each of all three streams**.
Correct unsupported STOPs must also be no fewer in every stream. If all conditions
hold, adopt the fixed PROTECTED policy for subsequent development under this new
contract, while explicitly retaining the earlier cohort's regression and failed
verdict. Otherwise keep RESERVED. Pooled gains cannot compensate a failed stream.
This rule is a development decision, not a significance test or language benchmark.

Report the absolute targets separately: at least 6/8 fresh edits, 12/16 retained
edits and both properties for three fresh entities in every stream. Appropriate
unsupported behavior remains required for the full integrated milestone; passing
the relative decision rule cannot hide wrong unsupported actions or establish
autonomous recovery, broad comprehension or any roadmap gate.

Budget: at most two hours for preparation/diagnosis starting **04:09 UTC**,
then one sequential execution per stream, at most thirty minutes total model
runtime. Run order is **wording, questions, competition**. Do not stop early for
a favorable or unfavorable score, alter later fixtures after an earlier result,
or extend this exercise beyond the three streams. A diagnosed infrastructure
failure must preserve its original evidence and receive a separately recorded
recovery decision, never silent replacement. Close this robustness comparison
with a capability decision regardless of outcome; do not start another round
of hand-picked recertification.

## Inputs and unchanged learning

| Stream | Fixed fresh-input pressure |
| --- | --- |
| `wording` | Varied sentence constructions, clause length, property order and passive phrasing in source facts; familiar request forms. |
| `questions` | Varied source constructions and semantically equivalent questions, including property paraphrases. |
| `competition` | Repeated entities/mentions, incidental property-word matches, irrelevant neighboring facts, source ordering and multiword values. |

Each `contextual_robustness_<stream>_cases.json` links the exact same openly reused
TRAIN fixture: sixteen requests, four documents, fixed 48-encounter schedule.
Each supplies six fresh documents and ten requests: eight supported requests
covering two properties for four entities, plus two unsupported requests. New
entity/value substitutions are disjoint across streams and from preceding
contextual comparison cohorts. Every supported target must have unambiguous
document support, a contiguous one-to-three-word candidate span, and no answer
disclosure in its request. The fixed Python literal-edit motor is unchanged.
These authored development examples are not independent reading-comprehension
evidence and must not substitute for authentic examinations.

Use the complete learning, numerical, custody and control contract from the
[protected comparison](contextual_protected_contrast_protocol.md), including
the [reserved allocation](contextual_reserved_feedback_protocol.md): BINDING
gain 16, fixed rate one, at most 150 pair offers, all 48 teacher executions,
reserved future correction opportunities and distinct cyclic replay. Actual
FAIL/teacher-PASS feedback and the existing post-STOP exploration remain unchanged.
No rate, representation, cell, projection, basis capacity, replay priority,
teacher schedule, source-selection heuristic or motor change is allowed.

RESERVED uses protection `none`; PROTECTED uses `orthogonal_certified`, capacity
48. Each has a same-policy, same-seed frozen mate with matching certified exposure
and zero learned state. Both arms use affine currents and interpolated GLIF as
the primary action selector. Retain all eleven existing evaluation policies,
including external and raw-score references and the predeclared ablations.
All references share the learned motor; they isolate evidence selection rather
than the whole neuronal system. The external frozen MiniLM encoder, raw sources,
candidate vectors and feedback ledger remain explicitly counted. The TRAIN host
feasibility witness is excluded.

## Evidence and interpretation

Each stream starts from the same initial states and repeats identical training.
Verify within each policy that trained synapses, auxiliary arrays and certification
records match across streams and the completed protected comparison before
attributing differences to fresh exposure. A mismatch is an unresolved integrity
failure, not a new scientific result. The two policies may have different
on-policy teaching ledgers, as in the original comparison.

These are three fresh-input/source contexts, **not independent learning
replications**. Retention repeats the same sixteen old queries under three stores;
do not report forty-eight independent acquired skills. Any pooled fresh/retained
totals are descriptive, alongside every stream's scores and paired gains/losses.

Save same-weight old-query scores/ranks before and after fresh ingestion, and
execute all existing transfer/retention policy actions: 572 per stream, 1,716
across the comparison. Inspect actual source absence at action time, decoys,
selected versus materialized edits, frozen learning, read-only inference and
checkpoint lineage. Internal retained raw documents remain disclosed: absence
from live input is not raw-source erasure or semantic compression.

Report uncertified admitted negatives, unexecuted competitors, fresh-source
displacement, protected-margin preservation and genuine projection stalls
separately. Exhausted projections whose original gate is already satisfied are
not blocked new learning. Record finite-budget acquisition failures as such;
do not infer that more steps or a higher rate would solve them without evidence.

Retain one original KS2 Q6a/b exam-helper call per arm per stream, with official
wording/rubric unchanged and no feedback. These are repeated integration
diagnostics, not fresh exam items, and cannot determine policy adoption or be
pooled as independent reading evidence. Pending official human marks stay pending.
New official development passage groups require separate selection and a frozen
manifest under the [reading plan](../../docs/research/reading-comprehension-evaluation.md).

Count runtime, parent memory scope, source/candidate bytes, learned and frozen
auxiliary state, scratch, encoder and external ledger payload without adding
overlapping measurements. Equal offer counts are not equal compute. Report
per-stream and total run costs, including any failed attempts separately.

Before execution, use the existing actual-source synthetic serialization seam,
fixture validation, early output-collision rejection and option-forwarding checks.
Check the fixed action schema can carry the new multiword values without model
preview. No core/native source changes means the verified native suites need not
be repeated. Commit reviewed source and all fixtures first; record source, native,
encoder and saved-array identities. Independently audit saved results without
model reruns. The active plan owns the resulting next capability priority.
