Free responses use simulated agreement and competition on the actual raw
observation, causal prefix and selected memory context. The inherited B/C/U and
codec remain unchanged; no backward error transport or separate executor fit
occurs. Action heads likewise update from their own activity and local errors.

and preserves a real continuation state. Full sequence teaching now reaches EOS
and uses separate context storage, but correct composition and action selection
remain unresolved. Further work must address those shared failures and measured
costs, with new learning opportunities and independent transfer pressure. No
extra round on this exposed stream or automatic byte/operation gate is selected.
