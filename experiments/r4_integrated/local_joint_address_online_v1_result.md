# Joint-address online v1: cost rejected before acquisition

The disposable cost workload and independent saved-evidence audit pass, but the
complete projection is **1,056.720 seconds**, above the fixed 900-second ceiling.
No 4,096-lesson acquisition ran. The new local forward-feature circuit remains
implemented; this cost result establishes neither preserved learned capability
nor the twenty-check completion gate.

## Decision and next engineering step

Close this cost attempt without fewer lessons, an extended time ceiling or a
different learning rule. The separately scaled action maxima contribute about
771.193 seconds of the projection; sampled complete action phases take roughly
85-105 ms, versus 13-19 ms for model phases. This locates the dominant measured
cost in the action path, without identifying which subprocess/startup operation
causes it.

Review the established Windows CPython spawn strategy that launches the base
interpreter while preserving the virtual environment through
`__PYVENV_LAUNCHER__`. It may avoid redirector startup overhead. Require paired
authored output/environment checks and a freshly versioned complete cost before
claiming an improvement. The next cost must also explicitly measure an accepted
motor action; supplied engineering probes remain separate from neuronal output
and do not count as learner successes. Keep the neuronal rule, exposure and
same-instance learning/action sequence fixed.
